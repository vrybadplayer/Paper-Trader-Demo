# Retail FOMO Behavior

## Definition
Fear of Missing Out (FOMO) in retail trading refers to the emotional drive that pushes individual investors to make impulsive decisions based on the fear that others are profiting from an opportunity they are missing.

## Characteristics
- Sudden surge in buying volume driven by social media hype.
- Ignoring fundamental analysis in favor of price momentum.
- Increased volatility during periods of high social sentiment.
- Tendency to chase pumps without proper risk management.

## Indicators
- Spike in retail trading volume (e.g., Robinhood, Webull inflows).
- Abnormal increase in social media mentions (Twitter, Reddit, StockTwits).
- Divergence between price action and underlying fundamentals.
- High put/call ratio skew indicating speculative buying.

## Typical Patterns
1. **Accumulation Phase**: Smart money accumulates quietly.
2. **Trigger Event**: News or social media catalyst.
3. **FOMO Spike**: Retail rushes in, driving rapid price increase.
4. **Distribution Phase**: Smart money exits as retail chases higher prices.
5. **Mean Reversion**: Price collapses as buying pressure dries up.

## Risk Considerations
- FOMO-driven moves are often short-lived and prone to sharp reversals.
- Position sizing should be conservative during detected FOMO episodes.
- Consider contra-trend strategies after confirming exhaustion signals.
- Monitor options flow for signs of excessive speculation.

## Mitigation Strategies
- Use sentiment indicators as a contrarian signal when extreme.
- Implement pre-defined entry/exit rules to avoid emotional decisions.
- Scale into positions gradually rather than all-in on breakouts.
- Keep a watchlist of stocks prone to retail-driven moves for potential fade strategies.
